FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on a call.

Do you build the website, or do I?

We do. Our team designs, writes and builds your website, then connects it to the CRM. You review and approve; we handle the rest, including hosting and ongoing updates.

Which countries do you work with?

We work with businesses in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Singapore and beyond. Local phone numbers are available in 100+ countries, and our team works across time zones.

Is the WhatsApp integration official?

Yes. We use the official WhatsApp Business API with a verified business profile, so your messages are compliant and your number won't get banned for bulk messaging.

Will the AI agent sound robotic to my customers?

The agent uses natural, human-like voices and is trained on your business. It discloses that it's an AI assistant, answers questions, and passes the lead to a person whenever they ask or the lead is ready to buy.

Can I keep my existing website or CRM?

Yes. We can connect your current site's forms to our CRM, or import contacts from tools like HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho or spreadsheets. Most teams switch fully once they see everything in one place.

How long does setup take?

CRM, calling and WhatsApp are usually live within a week. A new website takes about 14 days from kickoff, depending on how quickly content is approved.

Is there a contract?

No long-term lock-in. Plans are billed monthly and you can cancel anytime. Annual billing is available at a discount.
